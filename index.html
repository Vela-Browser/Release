A calm, customizable browser for your desktop

Vela is a desktop web browser for Windows. Make it look and feel the way you like, keep your passwords safe, block ads, get quick help from an assistant and chat with friends, all in one place.

What Vela does

Profiles

Keep browsing for work, home and friends separate, each with its own picture, banner, bookmarks and history. Profiles can be protected with a password or authenticator code.

Make it yours

Pick a theme and accent colour, switch the layout, tab style, fonts and spacing, or apply a quick look in one click.

Ad blocker

A built-in ad blocker helps keep pages cleaner and faster.

Password manager

Save and fill logins from an encrypted vault on your own computer.

AI assistant

Ask questions, summarize the page you are reading, and attach images or files for help.

Social

Follow people, become friends, send messages and create group chats with invite links.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Vela is built to keep your browsing on your own computer. This page explains what information the app and this website handle.

Information stored on your device

Your profiles, bookmarks, history, settings, saved passwords and downloads are stored locally on your computer. Saved passwords are kept in an encrypted vault. We do not receive or collect this data.

AI assistant

When you use the AI assistant, the text you type, any files or images you attach, and (only if you turn it on) the content of the page you are reading are sent to the AI service that provides the answers, so it can respond. Do not send sensitive information to the assistant if you are not comfortable with that.

Social features

Social features are optional. If you create a Vela Social account, your username, display name, profile picture and banner, bio, follows, friends and messages are stored on the Vela Social server you connect to. Whoever runs that server can access this data. Do not share passwords or other secrets in messages.

Advertising

Vela may show clearly labelled sponsored content on its start page, supplied by third-party advertising networks. These networks may collect technical information such as your IP address, device and browser details, and may use cookies or similar technologies, under their own privacy policies. You can hide sponsored content in the browser settings. We do not sell your browsing history, because we do not collect it.

This website

This website is a simple informational page. It may show advertising from third-party networks, which may collect the technical information described above.

Children

Vela is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this section.
